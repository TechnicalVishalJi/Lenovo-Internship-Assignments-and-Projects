# Web Development Final Project: FoodBridge

## 1. Selected SDG and Reason for Selection
**Selected SDG:** Goal 2: Zero Hunger

**What the SDG focuses on:**
Goal 2 aims to end hunger, achieve food security, improve nutrition, and promote sustainable agriculture. It focuses on ensuring that all people, especially the poor and vulnerable, have access to safe, nutritious, and sufficient food all year round.

**Why this issue is important:**
Globally, about one-third of all food produced for human consumption is lost or wasted, while millions of people go hungry every day. Addressing this imbalance is critical not only for human survival and dignity but also for environmental sustainability, as food waste is a major contributor to greenhouse gas emissions.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
**What is the problem?**
There is a massive disconnect between surplus food generated by local businesses (restaurants, supermarkets, bakeries) and the local shelters and NGOs struggling to feed vulnerable populations. 

**Where does this problem exist?**
This issue exists primarily in urban and suburban areas where commercial food operations produce daily surplus, yet local shelters face food shortages due to lack of real-time communication and logistics.

**Who is affected?**
It affects low-income families, homeless individuals, and anyone relying on food banks or shelters. 

**Why is it serious?**
It is serious because throwing away edible food while people starve nearby is a logistical and moral failure. It wastes resources (water, labor, land) used to produce the food and deprives people of basic sustenance.

**What could happen if it is not solved?**
If left unsolved, food insecurity will continue to rise, leading to malnutrition, poor health outcomes, and increased environmental degradation from rotting food in landfills.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution
**What your project is:**
FoodBridge is a full-stack web application designed to connect food donors directly with local NGOs and shelters.

**How the system works:**
Donors (restaurants or grocery stores) create an account and can instantly post details about surplus food (type, quantity, expiry time, pickup location). NGOs can log in to their dashboard, see a real-time feed of available food in their area, and click a button to "Claim" the food, which updates the status and notifies the donor.

**How it helps solve the problem:**
It eliminates the communication gap by providing a centralized, real-time platform where supply (surplus food) can immediately meet demand (shelters needing food).

**Who will use the application:**
1. **Donors:** Restaurant managers, grocery store owners, event organizers.
2. **Receivers:** NGO volunteers, shelter coordinators, food bank managers.

---

## 4. Project Features
1. **Role-Based Authentication:** Secure login and registration tailored for either Donors or NGOs.
2. **Donor Dashboard:** An interface for donors to easily post new food listings and track their donation history.
3. **Real-time Claiming System:** A live feed where NGOs can view available donations and claim them before they expire.
4. **Interactive UI:** A responsive, modern design ensuring usability across desktop and mobile devices.

---

## 5. Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** ReactJS, TailwindCSS, React Router DOM, Vite
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB (with Mongoose ORM)
- **Authentication:** JSON Web Tokens (JWT), bcryptjs
